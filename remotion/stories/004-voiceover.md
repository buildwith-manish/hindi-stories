# Short #4 voiceover — "Papa har Sunday meri purani cycle saaf karte the"

Same words as the captions in `004-papa-cycle.json`, same order as the images. Do not change the words, or the captions will no longer match the voice.

## ElevenLabs settings
- Model: **Eleven v3**.
- Voice: a **male** Hindi narrator, warm, young to mid age. The story is a son's, so a female voice would sound wrong on lines like "main saalon se ghar mein nahi tha".
- Stability: **Creative**. Speed: **1.0**.
- Generate 3 to 4 takes. Take extra takes of line 6.
- Export WAV with **no silence at the start** (the voice must begin on frame 1).

## Script (paste as one block)

```
[warm, matter-of-fact, a little nostalgic] पापा हर संडे मेरी पुरानी साइकिल साफ़ करते थे।

[quiet, distant, a little guilty] मैं सालों से घर में नहीं था।

[casual, curious] मैंने फ़ोन पर पूछा, "पापा, अब क्यों?" [gruff, brief, imitating Papa] वो बोले, "बस ऐसे ही।"

[slower, wondering] इस बार घर गया... [pause] साइकिल चमक रही थी। [softly, amazed] चेन में तेल था, टायरों में हवा।

[hushed, touched] जैसे मैंने कल ही चलाई हो।

[voice breaking, whispering] वो साइकिल नहीं साफ़ करते थे... [long pause] [softly, barely audible] वो मेरी वापसी की तैयारी करते थे।
```

## Line-by-line guide

| # | Image | Emotion | Target | Delivery note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Papa's hands wiping the chain | warm, matter-of-fact | about 3.2 s | Start instantly. Plant the question without sounding sad. |
| 2 | The empty courtyard (the cycle is gone from the frame) | quiet, distant | about 2.4 s | Short and a little guilty. Let it hang. |
| 3 | The cycle in the courtyard (the phone call is heard over home) | casual, then gruff | about 4.4 s | Two voices: curious son, then Papa, low and brief. Papa's "बस ऐसे ही" is the key beat. |
| 4 | The gleaming cycle | wondering | about 5.6 s | Slow down. A smile in "चमक रही थी". |
| 5 | Hand on the handlebar | hushed, touched | about 2.4 s | Almost to himself. |
| 6 | Papa on the step | breaking, whisper | about 4.4 s | Voice cracks on "तैयारी". Let the last word fade. |

Total voice about 22 s. The ending hits because lines 1 to 4 are calm and factual and line 6 is the first moment of real emotion.
